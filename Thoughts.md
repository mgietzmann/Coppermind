Resilience is the difference between agriculture as it exists today and a cultivated ecosystem - one perpetuates itself, the other, when left alone, dies. This is why cultivated ecosystems sound so interesting - they allow far less input from us and are generally sustainable. Whether they can produce enough is a big question, but if they could they’d simplify a lot of things. One awesome potential solution out of many.

So I’m interested in cultivated ecosystems for that reason.

However what this depends upon is understanding resilience. Resilience is ultimately the ability to buffer changes - to create a basin of attraction. What these basins look like, what they are formed from, etc. all requires a good understanding of the animal’s life cycle and what kinds of trials the species faces and how they deal with them - their reserves of resilience, their buffers. If these things are understood then we can cultivate the resilience in those ecosystems and also ensure we don’t topple that resilience through our actions.
