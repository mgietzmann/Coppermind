Nine populations listed under ESA [[Shirk 2023]]

Typically in depths <100m [[Sabal 2023]]

Known to change depth in search of thermal refugia (Hinke)[[Sabal 2023]]. They're expected to change in horizontal distribution with temperature changes and so perhaps this will happen with regards to vertical distribution as well [[Sabal 2023]]. 

Diel vertical migration may exist but the evidence is very variable [[Sabal 2023]]. 

Found patterns of thermal refugia for the southern populations [[Sabal 2023]]. They found fish were deeper during the night than during the day [[Sabal 2023]] However this was largely for southern fish and as they moved north the pattern got weaker and then vanished [[Sabal 2023]].
