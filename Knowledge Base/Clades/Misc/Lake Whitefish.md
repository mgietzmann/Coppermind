## Temperature/Thermoregulation

“The Lake Whitefish in our study were at consistently lowertemperatures than would be expected from laboratory studies.” (Bergstedt et al., 2016, p. 9)

“In July–September, when thermal stratificationwas in place in Lake Huron and behavioral thermoregulation possible, the mean temperatures occupied by Lake Whitefishranged from 8.7ºC to 10.2ºC, about 2ºC lower than laboratoryvalues for either preference or optimum growth” (Bergstedt et al., 2016, p. 9)
