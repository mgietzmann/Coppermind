Yellowfin tuna [Thunnus albacares (Bonnaterre, 1788)] (Hoolihan et al. 2014, Weng et al. 2009), bigeye tuna [Thunnus obesus (Lowe, 1839)] (Arrizabalaga et al. 2008), blackfin tuna [Thunnus atlanticus (Lesson, 1831)] (Fenton et al. 2015), blue marlin (Makaira nigricans Lacépède, 1802) (Goodyear et al. 2008, Holland et al. 1990, Kerstetter et al. 2003), and white marlin [Kajikia albida (Poey, 1860)] (Hoolihan et al. 2015) have all been reported to reduce diving activity at night with a majority of the nighttime spent in near-surface waters, which could indicate a reduction in feeding at night for these species (Kerstetter and Graves 2006).” [[Orbesen 2017]]
