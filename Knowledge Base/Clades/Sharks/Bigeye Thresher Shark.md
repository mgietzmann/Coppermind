Swordfish (Dewar et al. 2011, Lerner et al. 2013), bigeye thresher shark (Alopias superciliosus Lowe, 1841) (Weng and Block 2004, Musyl et al. 2011), and escolar [Lepidocybium flavobrunneum (Smith, 1843)] (Kerstetter et al. 2008) have been observed to spend daytime hours in the mesopelagic zone (>500 m) and vertically migrate to the epipelagic zone (<75 m) at night.
