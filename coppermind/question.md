# How have otter trawls been modified to improve selectivity and/or reduce bycatch?


## How do the modifications divide up?

The "zones" of an otter trawl are: (1) spreading mechanisms,  (2) entry point, (3) trawl body (sequentially smaller meshes), (4) extension and codend.

Kennelly 2021

7% of experiments in zone 1, 11% in zone 2, 11% in zone 3, with a whopping 71% in zone 4.

Kennelly 2021

There was no clear trend of more complex modifications being better than simpler ones. So starting simple and in the codend is likely best.

Kennelly 2021

## What kinds of modifications are done to zone 1?

Raising sweeps reduces bycatch of bottom dwelling species and optimizing the spread ratio can significantly help improve catches of target species.

Kennelly 2021

## What kinds of modifications are done to zone 2?

By removing the top rope, fish that rise are caught less without necessarily  losing fish that don't. (Haddock vs flatfish for example)

Kennelly 2021

Raising the foot rope reduces bycatch of flatfish whilst often maintaining catch rates of target species. This also can reduce benthic infauna and debris in the catch.

Kennelly 2021

Semicircular spreading ground gear improved catching efficiency for  larger cod and haddock while reducing drag.

Kennelly 2021

Removing tickler chains reduces catchs of elasmobranchs without affecting targeted haddock, whiting or flatfish. This takes advantage of herding behavior.

Kennelly 2021

LEDs (especially at night) can help with bycatch

Kennelly 2021

## What kinds of modifications are done to zone 3?

Increasing mesh size helps keep the fish you want and remove the fish you don't.

Kennelly 2021

Horizontal separator panels can help sort downwards oriented fish from  upwards rising ones.

Kennelly 2021

One can make "windows" of different shape or size mesh to allow certain kinds of  fish to escape.

Kennelly 2021

## What kinds of modifications are done to zone 4?

Reducing the circumference of the codend or length of extension can reduce bycatch.

Kennelly 2021

More flexible twine and single twine often improves selectivity.

Kennelly 2021

Simply replacing T0 with T45 mesh does wonders.

Kennelly 2021

Windows made of different kinds of mesh can be very useful here. You can also create windows to guide fish to those windows.

Kennelly 2021

Grids like the Sort-X and sort-V and flexigrids have been shown to be useful. However windows seem to be able to give the same bang  for significantly less hassle. However they remain quite popular

Kennelly 2021
