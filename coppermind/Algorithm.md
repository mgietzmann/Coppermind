## Getting Context

Context is found by answering questions. When you've run out of relevant questions you've found the context. 

Answering questions requires research. So to answer questions we must do research!

Questions link back to notes for answers and then provide summaries. So all of this becomes real straightforward. 

Seems like I should turn the categories of tags into questions to ask. 

One asks specific kinds of questions when building context for an introduction. Compare and contrast, put it work, value, and "adding to the knowledge" kind of questions. Therefore they pretty directly map to paragraphs you want. Therefore a lot of your structure arises as you just ask the questions. Then the research just fills in the blank. 

One goes specific to broad because the specific questions feed into the broad ones.
