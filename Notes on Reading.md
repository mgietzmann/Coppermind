## Using fluid dynamic concepts to estimate species movement rates in terrestrial landscapes

Bit of an odd paper. Treating fluid dynamics as just permeability and pressure is a different take than I’ve thought of before. I suppose the difference here is that one is trying to model the environment as opposed to really modeling the behavior. 

This almost works as a concept for me except for the fact that they leave pressure as a variable to be fit… which means the creatures aren’t really moving or behaving as a fluid at all. Instead one just has a model that is linear and that’s it.

I suppose what I’m more interested in is how to use the tools to describe fluids to describe animal behavior as well. This one is going in the bin.

## Migration as flow: using hydrological concepts to estimate the residence time of migrating birds from the daily counts

This one’s a keeper for sure. Very interesting way of using Bayesian ideas to sample for a specific function that gives you the information you want to know. Very very clever.
